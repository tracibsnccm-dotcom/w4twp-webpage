01 The Gap We Fill

EAP solves crisis. We solve the
days in between.

Every employer in America offers an Employee Assistance Program. Most employees don't use it. The reasons are predictable — stigma, awareness, accessibility, and the simple fact that traditional EAPs are built for acute mental health crisis, not the ordinary attrition of working life.

What gets missed: the periods that arrive without supplies, the toddler home with a fever, the night-shift worker rebuilding circadian rhythm, the wellness incentive that never gets redeemed because no one explained how.

These are not crises. They are predictable, recurring, addressable. And they are quietly costing organizations more than the EAP line item itself.

02 What We Do

A complete adjunct wellness program — not a replacement for your EAP.

Each W4TWP service is designed to sit alongside your existing benefits stack. We complement traditional EAP, primary care, and HR programs by addressing the practical, daily friction points that erode wellbeing and productivity before they ever rise to the level of crisis.

01
Mental Health Toolkit
Self-guided, evidence-based mental wellness resources employees can access without an appointment, a referral, or a stigma threshold. Built around the 4Ps of Wellness framework — Physical, Psychological, Psychosocial, Professional.
02
F.I.R.E. → A.S.H.
A proprietary stress-decompression methodology that helps employees move from acute activation (Frustration, Irritation, Rage, Exhaustion) to grounded recovery (Awareness, Stillness, Healing). Delivered through workshops, digital tools, and on-demand resources.
03
5 Wellness Strategies
A structured framework for organizational wellness planning that gives HR and Corporate Wellness leaders a defensible, evidence-based architecture instead of a scattered benefits menu.
04
Wellness Kits
Tangible, employee-distributed kits containing curated essentials — blood pressure monitor, thermometer, pulse oximeter, pill organizer, hot/cold pack, OTC medications, hygiene items, stretch band, water bottle, portable fan, and a discreet carrying tote — with educational guidance.
05
Feminine Hygiene & Menopause Support
Period poverty solutions, free in-restroom feminine hygiene programs, monthly coupon partnerships, and structured menopause workplace accommodations and education. Implementation guidance for any size organization.
06
Childcare Shuttle & Sick-Child Daycare
Coordinated childcare logistics that address two of the most underestimated drivers of unplanned absence: the daily commute and the sick-child day. Reduces absenteeism without expanding internal HR scope.
07
Wake-to-Work Programs
Structured onboarding and circadian support for shift workers, night-shift staff, and emergency responders. Bridges the gap between "you're hired" and sustainable performance.
08
Workshops & Training
Live and recorded sessions for HR teams, Corporate Wellness leaders, new-hire orientation, and monthly all-staff programming. Built to integrate with your existing learning and development infrastructure.

03 How We Work

Clinically grounded. Operationally realistic.

W4TWP is built on a clinical foundation — but designed for the realities of HR operations, procurement cycles, and cross-functional rollout. We don't sell software. We deliver programs that fit how your organization already works.

i.

RN-Led & Evidence-Based

Every program is designed and overseen by a registered nurse with 15+ years of clinical care management experience. Our frameworks — the 4Ps of Wellness, the 10-Vs of Care Management — are built on bedside reality, not vendor marketing.

ii.

Adjunct, Not Replacement

We complement your EAP, your benefits broker, and your existing wellness vendor stack. We do not require you to displace a single existing contract. Our scope is intentionally drawn to fill the gaps, not duplicate the room.

iii.

Implementation-First

Every offering ships with a defined implementation path — procurement language, rollout timeline, communication templates, and measurement criteria. You get a program, not a pitch deck.
