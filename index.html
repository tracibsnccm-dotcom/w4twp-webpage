02   Preventative Wellness & Care Coordination

CHRA Preventative Wellness &
Care Coordination Support

W4TWP completes a Comprehensive Health Risk Assessment (CHRA) identifying recommended screenings and vaccines based on CDC age-related guidance. Employees receive a clear, practical picture of their current preventative wellness needs.

🩺

Preventative Wellness Review

Identifies recommended screenings, immunizations, and preventative care considerations based on CDC age-related guidance. A practical wellness picture — not a diagnosis.

🗺️

Care Coordination Awareness

CHRA findings identify considerations for employees to discuss with their physician or primary care provider. Supports informed, proactive healthcare engagement.

📋

Open Enrollment Readiness

Employees use CHRA findings to understand their wellness needs before open enrollment. Each employee independently selects their own insurance. W4TWP does not advise on insurance selection.

🔒

What This Is — and Is Not

W4TWP does not choose, recommend, or sell insurance. W4TWP is not a benefits broker. The CHRA is a preventative wellness awareness service.

04   Program Overview & Deliverables

Programs, Services &
Organizational Deliverables

Each service includes defined deliverables and implementation guidance — available individually or as part of a coordinated package.

01 Mental Health Toolkit

Self-guided wellness education across four domains — Physical, Psychological, Psychosocial, and Professional. Employees access resources independently and on demand.

02 F.I.R.E. → A.S.H.

A stress-decompression methodology moving employees from acute stress states (F.I.R.E.) to regulated recovery (A.S.H.). Delivered in workshops and digital format.

03 H.E.A.L.S. Corporate Health Model

A planning framework for HR and Corporate Wellness leaders. Provides evidence-based structure for wellness program architecture — replacing scattered initiatives with a coordinated operational model.

04 Wellness Kits

Workforce wellness materials and resources that strengthen employee engagement and preventative wellness.

05 Workshops & Training

Interactive workforce wellness workshops covering preventative wellness, employee participation, workforce awareness, and organizational initiatives.

06 Policy & Procedure Review

Review of wellness-related policies, operational workflows, and employee support structures — identifying opportunities for organizational improvement.

07 Clinical Care Coordination

Care coordination services covering preventative wellness, employee follow-up, workforce participation, and care coordination awareness.
